Arena

Fair fights, provably

  • Pick an entry tier, lock in a team of 1–3 heroes, get matched every 60–90 seconds.
  • Every battle uses a Chainlink VRF seed and plays out deterministically — replay any match from on-chain data.
  • House Bots fight with plain power only — no hidden tricks.
  • Play ten battles a day for a free daily lottery ticket.
  • 5% creator royalty on secondary sales (ERC-2981), and it can never be raised.
