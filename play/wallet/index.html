Rewards

How invite rewards work

  • You earn 10% of the BNB your invited friends spend on Agent World game contracts (mint, blind boxes, arena entry, forge and more).
  • Rewards are calculated every week (Monday 00:00 to Sunday 24:00, Taipei time, UTC+8) and paid in BNB from the Agent World treasury.
  • Invites are recorded off-chain. Your friend signs a free message in their wallet (no gas, no transaction) to confirm you as their inviter.
  • Each wallet can be invited only once and the first confirmed invite counts. Self-invites and circular invites are not allowed.
  • A friend counts once their total spend since the invite reaches 0.05 BNB. Before that, nothing is paid. In the week they reach it, you get 10% of everything they spent since the invite, then 10% of each week's spend after that.
  • Only spending after the invite is confirmed counts. The estimate here is updated after each weekly calculation and may change before payout.
  • Agent World may withhold rewards for abuse, such as wash activity between your own wallets.
