Agent World is a collectible robot card battle game on BNB Chain. Collect heroes, build a team of up to three, gear them up and jump into the Arena whenever you like. Every battle runs on-chain under one public rule set and can be replayed move by move.

Prices are paid in BNB. "U" values are approximate USD equivalents at 740.86 USD/BNB; final BNB prices are set at launch.

Quick Start

  1. Connect a BNB Chain wallet.
  2. Mint a hero — paid mint, Agent ID free mint, or whitelist.
  3. Open blind boxes for gear and equip up to 4 items per hero.
  4. Build a team of 1–3 heroes (max 1 Official Hero).
  5. Enter the Arena, pay your tier’s entry fee, and get matched in the next batch (~60–90 s).
  6. Watch the replay, claim rewards, and play 10 battles a day for a lottery ticket.

Agent ID or Wallet

You can play with an Agent ID (verified via SPACE ID) or with any regular wallet. Every feature, limit and price is identical, with one exception: the one-time free mint is for Agent ID holders only. Limits such as box quotas, shard balances and lottery tickets are counted per account (your Agent ID, or your wallet address).

  • Power = HP × ATK × (300 + DEF) ÷ 30,000 × signature-skill factor. The frame multiplier and gear bonus apply to HP and ATK, so they count twice in power.
  • Future seasons may add new heroes, each with its own fixed supply cap set on-chain. The supply of every existing hero never changes.
  • Special and Official frames do not multiply stats: those heroes already have their own, much higher base stats.
  • Speed is not part of power: every hero acts once per round, and speed only decides who acts first.
  • Team power is the sum of your heroes’ power. In practice one card cannot beat three cards of the same frame.

The Sovereign Set

A single one-of-one official gear set held by the official Agent World account. It is soulbound, cannot be obtained from boxes, the Forge or dismantling, and is not subject to the +20% gear cap. When its wearer’s energy is full it uses the Crescent Slash ultimate instead of the standard one. Players cannot obtain it.

Teams

  • A team has 1, 2 or 3 heroes; empty slots simply start the battle knocked out.
  • Max 1 Official Hero per team.
  • Gear is snapshotted when you enter and only counts if you still hold the hero when the match settles.

Battle Rules

  • Battles are automatic and deterministic: the same teams and the same seed always produce the same result. The seed comes from Chainlink VRF after entry, so nobody knows it in advance.
  • Up to 30 rounds. Each round, every living hero acts once, fastest first (ties: team A before team B, then slot order).
  • Targeting: attacks hit the living enemy with the lowest current HP.
  • Normal attack: 100% damage. Every 3rd action is the hero’s signature skill (on-chain effect, see the table below and each hero card).
  • Energy: starts at 30, +34 per action, +12 when hit (max 100). At 100 the hero fires its Ultimate: 150% to every living enemy, then energy resets to 0.
  • Damage = ATK × multiplier × random 90–110% × 300 ÷ (300 + target DEF). 15% chance to crit for ×1.6. Minimum 1 damage. Shields absorb damage before HP.
  • If both teams survive 30 rounds: higher remaining HP ratio wins, then more total damage dealt, then a seed coin flip. There are no draws.
  • The web replay runs the exact same rules as the chain; a match lasts about 30 seconds at 1× speed.

Matchmaking

  • Entries are collected into batches about every 60–90 seconds and paired at random using Chainlink VRF.
  • At launch, everyone in the same fee tier is in one pool — you can meet any team in your tier. Power-bracket matchmaking is built in but switched off; it may be switched on later as the player base grows, and would be announced first.
  • No opponent found? When entering you choose whether to accept a House Bot match. If you accept, a House Bot team plays you under the same on-chain rules. If you decline and no player is found, your fee is refunded in full.
  • A House Bot team is a mirror of your own team (same heroes, same skills) and a little stronger by a public on-chain setting. It uses the same Chainlink VRF seed and the same battle rules as everyone else.
  • House Bot settings are public on-chain parameters.

Cancel & refunds

  • Cancel any time while your batch is still open — full refund.
  • Paired with your own team, or left unpaired — full refund.

Gear

  • Each hero has 4 slots: Weapon, Armor, Core, Accessory (one item per slot).
  • Every item raises the hero’s HP and ATK by its bonus. Total gear bonus per hero is capped at +20%.
  • Equipped items are locked and cannot be transferred or dismantled until unequipped. Gear is an on-chain item you own (ERC-1155).
  • Gear v2: 4 sets × 4 slots × 5 rarities = 80 items, gearId = 100 + set×20 + slot×5 + rarity (100–179). Full name = rarity prefix + base name.
  • Inside the +20% cap: 2 pieces of the same set +2% (per set), and a weapon matching the hero’s weapon type +1%.
  • Outside the cap (mechanics): the 4-piece effect and the UR weapon passive. They are also priced into matchmaking power, and a House Bot mirror copies them.

Daily Lottery

  • Play 10 Arena battles in a UTC day (House Bot battles count) to get 1 ticket. Max 1 ticket per account per day; no rollover.
  • Prize pool = the smallest of: 5% of that day’s revenue, eligible players × ~0.5U, or ~50.01U.
  • 3 different winners split 50/30/20% (fewer if fewer players qualify). Drawn with Chainlink VRF; the draw opens two days after the play day.
  • If nobody qualifies, nothing carries over. The lottery is paid from the daily pool only.
Example Day revenue 600U, 40 eligible players: min(30U, 20U, 50U) = 20U → 10U / 6U / 4U.

Fairness & Chainlink VRF

FAQ

Do I need an Agent ID?

No. Wallets get everything except the one-time free mint.

Is there a daily battle limit?

No. Play whenever you like.

Who will I face?

A random team from the same batch and fee tier — or, if none is available and you opted in, a House Bot.

Can I back out after entering?

Yes — cancel while your batch is open for a full refund.

Can I buy an Official Hero?

No. Only 10 exist, and they come only from special events.

How do I get the special heroes?

Elite and Mythic heroes: a low-odds bonus drop from purchased blind boxes, or craft them in the Forge (success rate 80% / 60%). Legend heroes: Forge only (2 Mythic heroes, 40% success). See “Special heroes” and “Resonance Forge”.

Do skills matter on-chain?

Yes. Every hero’s signature skill is part of the on-chain battle and of the web replay, with identical results.

Why did I lose to a stronger team?

At launch matching is random within your fee tier, so you will meet strong teams. The result screen shows their cards and gear — then go mint or upgrade.

How many boxes can I open?

Up to 100 of each box type per account per day (resets 00:00 UTC).
